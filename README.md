### `// building`

**[Moonbase AI](https://moonbase.biz/)** &nbsp;—&nbsp; Technology Commercialization Platform

A production AI platform serving university Technology Transfer Offices. Moonbase ingests NSF/NIH grant data, scores research across dimensions like market opportunity, IP landscape, PI credibility, and technology readiness, then surfaces actionable commercialization intelligence for TTO analysts.

> `AWS ECS Fargate` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `PostgreSQL + pgvector` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Neo4j` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Next.js` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `AWS Bedrock` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `RAG Pipelines` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Docker`

&nbsp;

**[Stargazer](https://stargazer.moonbase.biz/)** &nbsp;—&nbsp; Grant Intelligence Engine

Stargazer scans and scores NSF & NIH awards at scale — identifying which grants have the highest commercialization potential using AI-driven multi-dimensional scoring across market opportunity, IP white space, licensee availability, and PI credibility.

> `Python` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `AWS ECS` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `PostgreSQL` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `pgvector` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `RAG` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `AWS Bedrock` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Neo4j`
